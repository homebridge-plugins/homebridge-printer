## v1.0.0 (2026-11-09)

### Added

- First release, from @Wazza151's rewrite of SeydX's homebridge-printer, which works on Homebridge 2
- A Printing switch, an Online switch, and a printing sensor with fault status
- One tile per ink or toner cartridge, with a low-ink alert, and Eve print history
- Estimated watts and kWh over Matter, or everything over Matter only
- A live dashboard on the settings page and at port 8587, and a search for printers on the network
